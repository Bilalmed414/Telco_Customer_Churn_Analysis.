# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

**Executive Summary & Exploratory Data Analysis**  
**Dataset**: Telco Customer Churn (7,043 records)

---

## 📋 Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and **Executive Summary** of telecom customer churn using a dataset of 7,043 customers. The analysis identifies key drivers of customer attrition and provides actionable business recommendations to improve retention and reduce revenue leakage.

**Churn Rate**: **26.54%** (1 in 4 customers leaves)

---

## 🎯 Key Insights

- **Month-to-Month contracts** are the strongest predictor of churn — customers on flexible plans have significantly higher attrition.
- **Fiber Optic** subscribers (premium segment) show the **highest churn rate**, indicating potential issues with pricing, reliability, or perceived value.
- **Senior Citizens** (16.21% of base) churn at nearly **twice the rate** (~41%) of non-seniors.
- **New customers** (0–12 months tenure) are the most vulnerable.
- **Electronic Check** payers and customers without add-on services (Security, Tech Support, Backup, etc.) exhibit higher churn.
- Customers with **partners/dependents** and **longer contracts** are far more loyal.
